Privacy Policy — VEXO Player (IPTV and Video player)

Package ID: com.pureplayer.video  |  Developer: Ahmed M Moumen  |  Contact: AHMEDAHMED5547@GMAIL.COM  |  Last Updated: November 18, 2026

VEXO Player — IPTV and Video player operates on a strict 100% local-first privacy model. We do not collect, transmit, store on external servers, sell, or share any personal or sensitive user data.

1. Permissions & Local Media Access

  • Video Storage Access (READ_MEDIA_VIDEO / READ_MEDIA_VISUAL_USER_SELECTED): Used strictly on-device as core functionality to scan, organize, and play your local video folders and manage your Private Vault.
  • Biometric Authentication (USE_BIOMETRIC): Handled locally by the Android OS hardware and AndroidKeyStore to unlock Private Vault folders.
  • Internet Access (INTERNET): Used only when streaming user-supplied network URLs/playlists or fetching subtitles at the user's request.

2. Hardware Encryption & Backup Protection

  • Private Vault PIN: Hashed locally with PBKDF2WithHmacSHA256 (>= 100,000 iterations) and a 16-byte cryptographic salt.
  • Playlist Credentials: Encrypted on-device with AES-256-GCM backed by hardware AndroidKeyStore.
  • Backup Exclusion: All databases, media caches, and preferences are excluded from cloud auto-backup and device transfer.

3. Content Disclaimer

VEXO Player (IPTV and Video player) is a media player only and does not provide, bundle, host, or distribute any media content or playlists. Users are solely responsible for the content they add.
